# 08 — SEO and Content Analysis

## Homepage evidence

- Title: `Cynbit Technologies | AI Automation & Software Solutions Jaipur | Cynbit Technologies`.
- Meta description was present and described AI automation, autonomous agents, and digital architecture in Jaipur.
- Open Graph title and description were present.
- `lang="en"` was present.
- A canonical link was not found in the inspected homepage DOM.
- `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` returned HTTP 200.

## Content observations

- Homepage had one observed `h1` and multiple section headings.
- Case-study index had one observed `h1` and a separate `h2` for every study card, which is semantically reasonable from the inspected DOM alone.
- Case-study card alt text is descriptive; the underlying imagery is generic stock photography served from Unsplash.

## Limits

Robots and sitemap body contents, canonicals across routes, indexing directives, structured data, and exhaustive SEO link coverage are Not Tested. The follow-up sampled [public link destinations](09-edge-case-findings.md) and [responsive layouts](05-responsive-findings.md).
